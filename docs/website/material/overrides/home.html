PyMOL-Open-Source


PyMOL(TM) is a molecular graphics system with an embedded Python interpreter designed for real-time visualization and rapid generation of high-quality molecular graphics images and animations. It can also perform many other valuable tasks (such as editing PDB files) to assist you in your research. PyMOL is a USER-SPONSORED molecular visualization system on an OPEN-SOURCE foundation.


Available for Windows, macOS and Linux.

What is Open-Source PyMOL?

Ray-Tracing for Photorealistic Molecular Visualization Unlock unparalleled clarity with PyMOL’s advanced ray-tracing engine, designed to generate stunning, publication-ready 3D renderings. Showcase intricate molecular details—from electrostatic surfaces to binding pockets—with lifelike lighting, shadows, and depth, ensuring your visuals stand out in high-impact journals and presentations.

Seamless Export of Publication-Quality Imagery Streamline your workflow with one-click presets and customizable export options tailored to journal requirements. PyMOL delivers ultra-high-resolution images with anti-aliasing, and transparency controls, guaranteeing crisp, print-perfect figures for manuscripts, grants, and posters.
