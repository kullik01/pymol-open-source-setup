Open-Source PyMOL - third-party notices
=======================================

This application is an unofficial build of Open-Source PyMOL. Besides PyMOL it
contains Python and other open-source components. Their license texts are in
this folder; README.txt lists every bundled Python package with its version
and license.

PyMOL(TM) is a trademark of Schrodinger, LLC. This build is not provided,
